This page represents a curated list of combat robotics resources. If something is out of date or you'd like something added, just let Midnight Robotics leadership know.

# All-Encompassing Guides
## Beetleweights for Dummies
[(Link to Google Doc)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1IxulfzmkxP2UOKo1TPsEDITqoBg-wLu-mH5bXBPuA1U/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.lv8zpu7wqzvc)<br>
This is your first and likely last stop for most questions. While it's written for Beetleweight most concepts apply to all weight classes, particularly the Ant and Featherweight that we currently also compete in.<br>
Started in the summer of 2025 by University of Toronto student Leo Ping, if you feel like it's missing something you can contact him directly (details in doc) or join the [Collegiate Robot Combat Chat](https://discord.com/invite/c92gM7m5dd) to discuss it further. It's recommended you use the document tabs to navigate.

## Beetleweight Combat Robot Design Handbook (Rose-Hulman Institude of Technology)
[(Link to website to view and/or download)](https://repeat-robotics.com/handbook/)<br>
Written in 2020, most concepts still apply and the it's a bit more succinct compared to the Beetleweight for Dummies doc. Be sure to check out the "Further Resources" section as well.<br>
Written by then-student Peter Garnache (now owner of Repeat Robotics), this used to be the go-to resource for the club.

# Combat Robotics Communities
## Collegiate Robot Combat Community (CRCC)
[Discord Invitation Link](https://discord.com/invite/c92gM7m5dd)
Started in February 2023 by Griffin Hill (2nd President of Midnight Robotics), currently unites over 250 students across over 30 states and over 50 clubs, mostly US-based, to share resources and discuss combat robotics in the collegiate circuit. Midnight Robotics members are encouraged to join!
## Norwalk Havoc Robot League (NHRL) [Norwalk, CT]
[Discord Invitation Link](https://discord.gg/uyJHRS3jbk)
The largest hobby-level combat robotics organization in the US. Founded in 2018, hosts Beetle, Hobby, Sportsman Hobby, and Lightweight. Has grown exponentially, offered $2,000,000 in giving late 2022, half for the winner of each weight class to allocate to a charity and the other to 100 collegiate robotics orgs (of which we won one of the $10,000 grants!).
## Midwest Robot Combat Association (MRCA) [Midwest]
[Discord Invitation Link](https://discord.gg/Jrty5CQVAE)
Started ~2021 to unite the growing midwestern scene lead by Wisconsin Robot Combat (since merged into MRCA) founder Joseph Betz, alters some rules from NHRL. Still actively growing.
